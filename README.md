### 🧠 Business Problem
The company aimed to enhance customer satisfaction but lacked clarity on how satisfaction varied by income levels, countries, loyalty tiers, and purchasing behavior.

### 📊 Data & Tools Used
- **Data Size:** Survey feedback from multiple regions
- **Tools:** `Alteryx`, `Excel`

### 🔍 Approach
1. Explored and segmented data by income, purchase frequency, loyalty tier, and country.
2. Performed correlation analysis to uncover drivers of satisfaction.
3. Conducted gender-based and regional satisfaction trend analysis.
4. Compiled actionable insights with data-driven business recommendations.

### 💡 Key Findings
- **Service Quality (0.55)** and **Product Quality (0.54)** were the strongest satisfaction drivers.
- Low-income, occasional buyers and UK Gold-tier customers had the **lowest satisfaction**.
- **Germany** had the highest satisfaction levels; the **UK** had the lowest.
- Gender-based variation was minimal but showed **higher female satisfaction** in some regions.

### 📈 Impact & Outcome
- Guided loyalty program changes for underperforming segments.
- Suggested service quality training and support improvements.
- Estimated **10–15% potential uplift** in satisfaction by focusing on key low-performing areas.
